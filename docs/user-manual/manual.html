1Introduction to MathPulse AI

1.1What is MathPulse AI?

MathPulse AI is a learning app for Senior High School STEM mathematics. It follows the DepEd Strengthened SHS curriculum and gives every student a 24/7 AI tutor, guided lessons, and games that reward practice. Teachers see who needs help early, and administrators keep the school's data, curriculum, and AI usage in order.

Note: MathPulse AI runs in any modern web browser and can be installed on a phone like a regular app. No separate download store is needed.

1.2Intended users

Students

Grade 11–12 STEM learners who study modules, take quizzes, chat with the AI tutor, and earn XP.

Teachers

Math teachers who manage classes, build AI quizzes, import grades, and track at-risk students.

Administrators

School staff who manage accounts, sections, curriculum files, AI health, and audit logs.

1.3Key features

L.O.L.I. AI Tutor

Step-by-step help, any time.

Curriculum Modules

DepEd lessons, materials, and quizzes.

Diagnostic Assessment

Finds strengths and gaps on day one.

Quiz Battle

1-vs-1 matches with classmates or a bot.

XP, Streaks & Ranks

Levels, leaderboards, and avatar rewards.

At-Risk Monitoring

Early warning for students who need help.

AI Quiz Maker

Teachers generate quizzes in minutes.

Curriculum Knowledge Base

AI answers grounded in DepEd modules.

3Installation & Deployment Guide

3.1Open MathPulse AI in a browser

  1. Open your browser (Chrome on Android or computer, Safari on iPhone/iPad).
  2. Go to https://mathpulse-ai-2026.web.app
  3. Sign in with the account your school gave you (see Section 4).

3.2Install the app on your phone or computer

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install. MathPulse AI appears on your home screen.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. 1Tap the phone icon at the top of the app.
  2. 2Follow the guide: Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.

Computer (Chrome / Edge)

Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, then Install.

Note: The installed app updates itself. When a new version is released, it loads automatically the next time you open MathPulse AI.

3.3Deployment guide (IT staff)

These steps publish MathPulse AI for a school. They need a computer with Node.js 22, Git, and the Firebase CLI, plus access to the mathpulse-ai-2026 Firebase project.

  1. Get the code and install packages
    git clone <repository-url> && cd MATHPULSE-AI npm install
  2. Add configuration. Copy .env.example to .env.local and fill in the Firebase and AI keys. Keep the Firebase service-account file in .secrets/. Never commit either one.
  3. Build and publish the web app
    npm run build npx firebase deploy --only hosting --project mathpulse-ai-2026
  4. Publish Cloud Functions (notifications, scoring, Quiz Battle)
    cd functions && npm install && npm run build npx firebase deploy --only functions --project mathpulse-ai-2026
  5. Publish the backend API. Pushing changes under backend/ to the main branch syncs them to the Hugging Face Space automatically. Set the Space secrets FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON, DEEPSEEK_API_KEY, DEEPSEEK_BASE_URL, and INFERENCE_PROVIDER.
  6. Optional: build the Android app
    npm run android:release
  7. Check the release. Open the site and sign in once as a student, a teacher, and an administrator.

Note: Only IT staff need this section. Students and teachers just open the link or install the app (Section 3.2).

4System Access & Authentication

Everyone signs in on the same screen. MathPulse AI opens the right home screen for your role automatically.

4.1Signing in

STEPS TO SIGN IN:

  1. 1Type your email address and password, then tap Sign In. Tap the eye icon to show the password.
  2. 2Forgot it? Tap Forgot password? (see 4.2).
  3. 3Using a Google account from your school? Tap Continue with Google.

Note: Your school administrator creates teacher and administrator accounts. Students may also create their own account (4.2).

Security: Sign-in is protected by TLS. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

4.2Creating an account & resetting a password

Create an account

On the sign-in screen, tap Create one.

  1. 1Enter your full name and choose Student or Teacher.
  2. 2Students pick their grade level, section, and track so they join the right class.

Add your email and a password, then tap Create Account.

Reset your password

  1. 1Type your account email and tap Send reset link.
  • Open the email from MathPulse AI and choose a new password.
  • Tap Back to sign in and sign in with the new password.

Note: No email after a few minutes? Check your Spam folder.

4.3Your home screen by role

After signing in, each role lands on its own dashboard.

Signing out

  1. 1Students: tap Profile on the bottom bar, then Sign Out.
  • Teachers & administrators: tap your photo at the top-right corner, then Sign Out.

Tip: Always sign out on shared or school computers.

6Troubleshooting & Common Issues (FAQ)

Try these quick fixes first. If a problem continues, contact your teacher or school administrator.

I can't sign in.

  • Check the email spelling and that Caps Lock is off.
  • Use Forgot password? to set a new one.
  • Still blocked? Your account may be deactivated. Ask your administrator.

The reset email never arrived.

  • Wait 5 minutes and check Spam or Promotions.
  • Make sure you typed the email your account uses.

The app is stuck on "Loading…".

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Close and reopen the app, or refresh the page.
  • Update your browser to the latest version.

L.O.L.I. says the AI tutor is unavailable.

  • The AI service is busy or offline for a moment.
  • Wait a minute and send your question again. Your chats are saved.

I don't see an install option.

  • iPhone: use Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: use Chrome → ⋮ menu → Install app.
  • Already installed? The option is hidden.

I'm not getting notifications.

  • Allow notifications for MathPulse AI in your phone or browser settings.
  • Turn on Alerts & Reminders in Settings.

My XP or streak didn't update.

  • Refresh the app; progress syncs when you are online.
  • A streak counts once per day, so finish at least one activity daily.

Quiz Battle can't find an opponent.

  • Few players may be online. Try VS Bot.
  • Or share a room code with a classmate.

A module is locked or "Coming Soon".

  • Your teacher or administrator has not opened it yet.
  • Ask your teacher when it will be available.

The screen looks too dark or too bright. All

  • Go to Settings → Display & Theme and choose Light or Dark mode.

My spreadsheet import failed. Teacher

  • Use a .csv, .xlsx, or .xls file.
  • Select the target class before uploading.
  • Keep the DepEd class-record layout (names and scores in their columns).

The AI gives off-topic answers. Admin

  • Open RAG Manager and check the pipeline is Online.
  • After uploading modules, tap Rebuild Knowledge.

I see an "offline" page.

  • Your device lost its connection. Reconnect and the app reloads.

A user can't see their class. Admin

  • Check the student's section in Users.
  • Check a teacher is assigned in Classes.

7System Maintenance & Security

7.1Built-in security

Secure sign-in

Firebase Authentication handles passwords. MathPulse AI never stores them.

Role-based access

Students, teachers, and admins only see what their role allows, in the app and on the server.

Encrypted data

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.

Audit trail

Admin and system events are logged and can be exported.

Rate limits

The API limits requests per role to stop abuse and runaway AI costs.

Private keys

API keys live in protected server secrets, never in the app or the code.

7.2Good habits for every user

  • Use a strong password you don't use anywhere else.
  • Never share your account or password.
  • Sign out on shared or school computers.
  • Lock your phone with a PIN or fingerprint.
  • Keep your browser and phone updated.
  • Report strange account activity to your administrator.

7.3Maintenance checklist (administrators & IT)

TaskHowHow often
Review security eventsInsights → Audit Log; check warningsWeekly
Refresh the knowledge baseAI → RAG Manager → Rebuild KnowledgeAfter new uploads
Update sections & teachersManage → ClassesEach semester
Clean up accountsManage → Users; deactivate graduates and transfereesEach school year
Back up the databaseFirebase Console → Firestore → Import/Export to a Cloud Storage bucketMonthly
Rotate secret keysReplace AI and service-account keys; update Space secretsEvery 6 months, or right away if exposed
Publish updatesFollow Section 3.3; test all three roles after releaseEach release

7.4Backup & recovery

  • Keep at least the last 3 monthly Firestore exports.
  • To restore, import an export from the Firebase Console.
  • Curriculum PDFs stay in Firebase Storage. Rebuild the knowledge base after restoring.

7.5Data privacy

  • Collect only what lessons need: name, LRN, grade, and section.
  • Give admin roles only to staff who need them.
  • Follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) for student records.

AAppendix A: Glossary of Terms & System Definitions

TermDefinition
XP (Experience Points)Points earned from lessons, quizzes, and battles. XP raises your level and buys avatar items.
LevelYour rank in the app. It goes up as your total XP grows.
StreakThe number of days in a row you completed an activity.
Daily GoalsThe number of lessons to finish each day.

Risk LevelA label (Low, Moderate, High) showing how much support a student may need, based on diagnostic results, grades, and in-app scores.
At-Risk StudentA student whose risk level means a teacher should step in.
InterventionExtra tasks or materials a teacher assigns to help an at-risk student.
L.O.L.I.Logical Operations & Learning Intelligence, the MathPulse AI math tutor and mascot.
Quiz BattleA live 1-vs-1 math match against a classmate or a bot.
LeaderboardRankings of students by XP (daily, weekly, all time).
TermDefinition
Avatar StudioWhere students customize their mascot with items unlocked by XP.
Student PassA student's digital school ID inside the app.

Topic MasteryA teacher view of class scores per topic and competency.
