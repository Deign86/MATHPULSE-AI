###### Key Terms and Definitions

*Scientific notation is a mathematical method used to* express very large or very small numbers in a more concise and manageable form. It is especially useful in scientific disciplines where such numbers are prevalent. It is being represented as:

##### m × 10ⁿ

where: m is a coefficient (also called the significand or mantissa) that is greater than or equal to 1 but less than 10 and n is an integer exponent indicating the power of 10 by which the coefficient is multiplied. This notation simplifies calculations and comparisons, especially when dealing with extremely large or small quantities.

To convert a number to scientific notation:

1. Move the decimal point to create a number between 1 and 10.
2. Count the number of places the decimal was moved; this becomes the exponent of 10.
3. If the decimal was moved to the left, the exponent is positive; if to the right, it's negative.

-----

Applying Scientific Notation to SARS-CoV-2 using scientific notation, let's consider the size of the SARS- CoV-2 virus:

Diameter: Approximately 60 to 120 nanometers (nm).

1 nanometer (nm) = 1 × 10⁻⁹ meters. Converting to meters:

60 nm = 0.00000006 meters = 6 × 10⁻⁸ meters. 120 nm = 0.00000012 meters =1.2 × 10⁻⁷ meters.

Expressing these measurements in scientific notation makes it easier to comprehend and compare the minuscule size of the virus. It is also more convenient for comparisons and mathematical operations, like multiplication and division.

Standard notation is the conventional way of writing numbers using digits, without exponents or powers of ten. It's the format we commonly use in everyday life, such as 4,500,000,000 or 0.00000036. In standard notation, each digit has a specific place value: units, tens, hundreds, etc., which influences the overall value of the number. This format is intuitive and straightforward for representing numbers in their usual form.

To convert scientific notation to standard notation:

1. Identify the exponent.
2. Move the decimal point in the base number according to the exponent. (If the exponent is positive, move the decimal point to the right; if negative, to the left.)
3. Write the result.

###### Activity B.2: Understanding and Applying Unit Conversion

Unit conversion is the process of changing one unit of measurement to another. It's important because different systems use different units, and we often need to convert between them.

Within the Same System (e.g., Metric System):

In the metric system, we convert by multiplying or dividing by powers of 10.

Example: 1 kilometer = 1,000 meters.

To convert 3 kilometers to meters:

3 km × 1000 = 3,000 meters.

1

Across Systems (e.g., Metric to English): To convert between different systems, we use conversion factors.

Example: 1 inch = 2.54 centimeters.

To convert 5 inches to centimeters: 5 inches × 2.54 = 12.7 centimeters.

1

Steps for Conversion: Identify the units. Find the conversion factor. Multiply or divide by the factor. Write the result with the correct units.
