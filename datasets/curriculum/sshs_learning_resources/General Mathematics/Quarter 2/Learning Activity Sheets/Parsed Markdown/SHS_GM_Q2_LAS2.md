## Part 4: Word Problems

Solve the following word problems involving scientific notation. Express your answers in scientific notation and include appropriate units.

1. Light travels at a speed of 3.00 × 10⁸ meters per second. The distance from Earth to the Sun is approximately 1.496 × 10⁸ kilometers. How many seconds does it take for light to travel from the Sun to Earth? Express your answer in scientific notation.
2. The diameter of the Sun is approximately 1.39 × 10⁶ kilometers, while the diameter of Earth is about 1.27 × 10⁴ kilometers. How many times larger is the diameter of the Sun compared to Earth? Express your answer in scientific notation.
3. The world population in 2025 is estimated to be 8.5 billion people. The population of a city is 4.2 × 10⁶ people. What is the ratio of the city's population to the world population? Express your answer in scientific notation.
4. The Alpha Centauri system is approximately 4.37 light-years away from Earth. A spacecraft travels at a speed of 2.5 × 10⁴ kilometers per second. If the spacecraft could travel directly to the Alpha Centauri system without stopping, how many seconds would it take to reach it? Express your answer in scientific notation. Note: 1 light-year ≈ 9.461 × 10¹² kilometers.
