##### Estimating Length and Distance Estimation is the process of making an approximate

calculation or judgment of a value, number, or quantity when an exact answer is not necessary or practical. We can estimate lengths and distances using approximate measurements. For example, one meter is about the distance from your shoulder to your fingertips when your arm is stretched out. It is also roughly the length of one big step or jump.

##### Measurement is a fundamental concept in mathematics that

deals with determining the size, length, or quantity of an object or space. To measure accurately, we use measuring instruments. Some examples are given below. A ruler usually shows measurements in centimeters and millimeters. Most rulers are either 15 cm or 30 cm long. They are commonly used to measure small objects, such as a tin can, or the length of a piece of paper. A measuring tape is marked with centimeters and meters. It is useful for measuring lengths of fabric, as well as large household objects such as furniture and rooms. A trundle wheel has a circumference of 1 meter. Basic Geometric and Measurement Terms Perimeter - the total distance around the boundary of a closed figure. Example: The length of the fence needed around a garden. Area - the amount of space a figure covers on a flat surface. Example: The number of tiles needed to cover a room floor. Length - the longer side of a rectangle or the measurement of how long something is. Width (or Breadth) - the shorter side of a rectangle; how wide something is. Height - vertical measure, especially in walls of rooms or fences. Dimensions - measurements (length, width, height) that describe the size of a figure or object. Units of Measurement - standard quantities used to measure (e.g., centimeters, meters, square meters, feet, square feet). • Linear units → used for perimeter (cm, m, ft) • Square units → used for area (cm², m², ft²) Shapes Common in Residential Spaces Rectangle - a quadrilateral with opposite sides equal and all angles 90°. Most rooms and gardens are rectangular. Square - a special rectangle with all sides equal. Example: a small patio or picture frame. Triangle - a three-sided figure, often seen in roofs or garden corners. Circle - a round figure, useful in layouts for circular tables, fountains, or round gardens.
