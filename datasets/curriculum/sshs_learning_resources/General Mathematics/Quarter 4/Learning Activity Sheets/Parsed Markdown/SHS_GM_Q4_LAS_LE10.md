**Objective: By the end of this lesson, learners are able to create a financial proposal using**

comparisons of different investment plans to recommend and explain the best financial strategy.

**Title: Building a Financial Proposal Through Smart Investing**

## Groupings:

- Learners will work in groups of 4-6 members.
- Each member has a specific role:
      1. Researcher (given data handler): Uses the provided investment data (interest rates, terms, etc.) as inputs.
      2. Mathematician/Analyst: Computes future value, present value, and interest based on the given data.
      3. Data Manager: Creates tables, graphs, and charts.
      4. Writer/Editor: Drafts the financial proposal with explanations.
      5. Presenter(s): Lead the delivery of the group presentation.

### Task Description:

As a team of financial consultants, you are tasked to design a financial proposal for a given client profile (see sample cases below). You will compare three investment plans using the provided investment data and recommend the best financial strategy.

## Sample Investment Data (Provided by Teacher)

*(These are only examples; teacher may adjust as needed)*

- Time Deposit: 3.5% annual interest, compounded annually, minimum ₱50,000, term 5

years

- Mutual Fund: Average return of 7% annually, compounded quarterly, moderate risk
- Pension Plan: 5% guaranteed annual growth, compounded annually, long-term (10+ years)
- Stocks (optional): 10% average annual growth, high risk, compounded annually

### Sample Client Profiles (Choose or Assign)

- Rosa, age 25, wants to start saving for her retirement at age 60.
- A young couple, both age 30, are saving for their child's college education in 15 years.
- Mark, age 40, wants to invest for a business expansion fund in 7 years.

## Process & Output:

1. Use the given investment data (no external research needed).
2. Compute maturity values, present values, and growth using simple/compound interest formulas.
3. Compare risks, returns, and suitability for the client.
4. Create a Financial Proposal (written report or digital presentation).
5. Present findings as a group (10-15 minutes).
